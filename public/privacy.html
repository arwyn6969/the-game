Your town and your data

Privacy information · Updated 27 October 2026

THE GAME is operated by Arwyn Hughes. For privacy questions or a request concerning your server account data, contact mrarwyn@gmail.com.

Your saved town

The town, its history and your dialogue preferences are saved in this browser. There is no automatic account-based town synchronization. Download town creates a portable backup that includes conversation history and private resident notes; keep it somewhere private. Clearing this site's browser data removes local saves, so export anything you want to keep first.

Optional Google sign-in

Google sign-in supplies your account identifier, name, email address and profile picture. THE GAME stores account and session records in its Neon database and uses necessary cookies to keep you signed in and protect authentication requests. It does not request access to your Gmail, Drive, contacts or Google password. Signing out ends your game session; it does not delete your local town.

AI dialogue

When AI is enabled, your message, relevant memories, recent dialogue and resident profiles are sent to the configured AI service to generate a reply. The Cloudflare deployment uses OpenRouter's free Nemotron route. OpenRouter and its model provider process these requests under their own policies. Avoid entering sensitive personal information into dialogue. The game records usage counters to enforce shared limits.

Choose Use local dialogue only on arrival or under Help & privacy to send no AI requests, including residents' background conversations. Local replies remain available when AI is unavailable or a quota is reached.

Read OpenRouter's privacy policy. Google sign-in is separate from the game's AI provider.

Your own AI source

Under Help & privacy, AI source lets you choose your own API key or an OpenAI-compatible model server on this device, such as Ollama or LM Studio. Messages and relevant resident context, including background AI while town time runs, go directly from this browser to the endpoint you select. Personal requests do not use the game's hosted allowance or server-side usage accounting. Your provider's billing, limits and privacy policy apply. A failed personal request does not switch to hosted AI.

The game keeps your API key only in this tab's memory, sends it only to your selected endpoint, and excludes it from browser storage and town backups. Re-enter the key after reload. Your selected source, endpoint and model name are saved separately in this browser. The local server must allow this game's origin; a browser may also require local-network permission. Choose a downloaded local model if you want inference on your device; a local server can itself be configured to forward requests to a cloud provider.

Optional wallet and stamp tools

Wallet actions require you to choose them explicitly. A verified address, signed proof and selected stamp information may be stored for your identity or private resident profiles. Public blockchain services are queried for relevant holdings, and Cloudflare processes public stamp artwork into display images. THE GAME does not receive wallet private keys. Funding, spending and trading are not enabled by these tools.

Hosting and service records

Cloudflare hosts the application, and Neon stores server-side account and feature records. These services process the network information needed to serve requests, such as IP addresses and request metadata. Operational logs and usage records support troubleshooting, security and quota enforcement. Local town storage and exported files are separate from these server records.

Your controls

You can use local dialogue, sign out, remove this site's browser data, or revoke the Google connection in your Google Account settings. To request access to or deletion of server records, email the contact above from your account address. Removing a Google connection alone does not delete existing game records or browser backups.
