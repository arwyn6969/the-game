Updated 2 November 2026. [Current status](CURRENT_STATUS.md) owns source and deployment truth. [The historical roadmap](ROADMAP_HISTORY_2026-11-02.md) preserves earlier scope and contracts.

## Now: a dependable week in town

The controls/work/meeting stack is merged; this source integrates the café and personal-model controls. Require passing combined-source CI before merging, then follow the three bounded acceptance packets:

| Order | Outcome                            | Evidence                                                                                                                                                                  |
| ----- | ---------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1     | A reliable, useful hosted activity | [#64](https://github.com/arwyn6969/the-game/issues/64): published revision, ordinary controls, truthful closure, preserved audiences/accounts and human/device acceptance |
| 2     | A café week people understand      | [#65](https://github.com/arwyn6969/the-game/issues/65): mixed work, spending and interruptions; conserved accounts; voluntary repeat choice                               |
| 3     | Qualified optional personal AI     | [#66](https://github.com/arwyn6969/the-game/issues/66): actual browser/provider/local-server compatibility and key privacy                                                |

The café remains an opt-in bounded pilot. Keep its unresolved acceptance contract visible. Neither test counts nor a 30-day quiet trace prove enjoyment or indefinite sustainability.

## Next: one persistent shared slice

Define canonical identity/continuity (A03), truth and private knowledge (A04), and compute/model replacement (A09) contracts. Then prove A05 for one resident, one object and one activity with synthetic inference: two clients, no connected browsers, duplicate delivery, process restart and coherent restore.

The existing `experiments/server-authority` and `experiments/server-adapter` are local prototypes with explicit storage, authority and privacy limitations. They are not a production server. Resolve navigation runtime state, stale-job policy, filtered views and transactional storage before full-town migration.

## Later, after those foundations

Deepen retained memory and recall (A06/A07), character decisions (A08), tested learning and tools (A10/A11), usable construction (A12) and research (A13). Sponsorship, shared-game proposals, self-understanding, donated compute and model training remain later or conditional work. Agent-owned apps/land and away-player AI retain the contracts in the historical roadmap and production plan.

Keep the current cast, portable saves, earned obligations and private knowledge intact. Public funding, outgoing real assets and external settlement retain their own requirements. There are no delivery dates or additional active coding lanes implied by this roadmap.
