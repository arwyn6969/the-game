# Character development and launch completion

Give residents recognisable starting characters, retained experience and opportunities to change through actual town life. The first completion target is nine residents whose voices remain distinct, whose existing histories survive the additions, and whose growth can be followed through a playable activity.

## Sources for the starting portrayals

- [Kevin Stamp lore](https://kevinstamp.com/lore): project origin narrative connecting developer Kevin, the portrait and the stamp persona. The user's intended connection is retained without settling the character's initial knowledge.
- [Sub's public profile](https://x.com/subterranean_1) and [his own account of his work](https://subterranean.xyz/about): digital artwork, books, creative history and practical writing. These inform the fictional resident's interests rather than generating memories of town events.
- Plato's [Apology](https://classics.mit.edu/Plato/apology.html) and [Symposium](https://classics.mit.edu/Plato/symposium.html), in the Jowett translations: the initial textual basis for Socrates. New dialogue is an adaptation, not quotation or a claim to have reconstructed the historical person exactly.
- [The Estée Lauder Companies account of its founder](https://www.elcompanies.com/en/who-we-are/our-heritage/our-founder): demonstrations, samples, customer attention and retail presentation. It is a corporate account; wider character research and review can refine the portrayal. No company or estate endorsement is claimed. Public name and likeness clearance remains a release decision.

## Compatibility and operating rules

New-town descriptions change starting conditions only. Loading an older town preserves existing identity prose, wants, memories, relationships, names and financial history. Socrates and Estée arrive at the town's actual load date with zero money; no historical attendance or friendship is granted. The opening total remains 90 coins in the standard town.

Pre-arrival v2 input is fully audited before adding newcomers. Existing world and character IDs remain unchanged; new IDs are reproducible within the same source world. New arrivals in an already forked world begin there without an invented ancestor. A successful first browser write retains exact pre-arrival bytes; failed backup and stale-tab writes preserve the canonical original.

The private registry continues to read complete older eight-person registrations. New ten-person registrations require `20261105_character_arrivals.sql`. That migration changes the permitted role names, not existing owners, addresses or profiles. Registering an expanded roster against an older private copy remains a conflict rather than remapping its profiles. No production migration is part of local validation.

Model routes, allowances, hosting and authority stay under their existing contracts. The larger cast uses bounded dawn batches and bounded prompt contexts. A character's historical reputation or confident prose cannot establish a sale, skill, payment or completed action.

## Following completion work

After the local candidate is qualified, conduct one short human cast review and the library growth activity. Use the results to choose a bounded next improvement: a second reusable method, richer relationship dimensions, or a player-visible growth history. Lifetime archives, general research/tools and browser-closed shared life keep the dependencies and acceptance criteria in the resident-development framework.
