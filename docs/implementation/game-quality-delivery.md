# Current game quality delivery

15 October 2026. This source candidate implements the current-game backlog from the [quality review](../reviews/2026-10-13-game-quality.md), with cross-device saving and a continuously running shared town explicitly deferred by the user. It also integrates main through `4a39cdf`, retaining the released character cards, conversation timeline, watch controls, per-resident drafts and remembered deeds. Publication is a separate action.

## Play the new loop

Start a fresh town and select local-only dialogue if desired. Open **Library** to help Northern arrange his first reading, read the work terms and choose source checking or reference checking. Refusal creates no player obligation or trust penalty. Use **Go to** to reach the library while time is paused.

Inspect the required public cards and boxes, submit the paid preparation report, and request the other funded report. Directly observed box locations carry into an empty, unsubmitted draft. Review them, optionally change them, or delegate submission to Arwyn. A loose historical card cannot auto-verify a current location; selected mistakes and submitted revisions are not overwritten. Unsupported mappings stay provisional. **Test this index** books Northern's independent paid lookup against the frozen revision; he walks to the selected box. The optional wait shows game minutes and existing commitments before advancing normal simulation.

Payment for completed agreed work and lookup usefulness are separate. A wrong reference can produce a failed, fairly paid search. A free **Find a record** interaction uses the submitted index; the town keeps access after funding closes. On later game days, the public result, attribution and a separately paid correction provide the aftermath. Sub can optionally publish verified entries only or clearly mark provisional entries.

After the actual lookup, choose a small park reading with Northern and Sub or a larger library reading with Northern, Arwyn and Sub. The latter needs a verified Cedar room reference and Baba's dated note. For newly accepted or explicitly adopted reading invitations, that note is due 30 game minutes after the lookup receipt; the disclosed wait advances ordinary simulation. Older projects without the optional reading extension keep their previous timing until the player explicitly chooses this new story.

**Go to the reading** walks to a dedicated free tile while named guests travel to distinct seats around prior paid work. A guest immediately beside an occupied seat in the same venue can also gather; a blocked distant route cannot manufacture attendance. Once everyone is physically there, join for free to listen or explicitly volunteer the quoted first line. After 60 ready game minutes with the player elsewhere, the guests may go ahead; Sub's public note states the player's absence without a fee or trust penalty. Standing beside the guests keeps the participation choice available. Closing the paid project does not cancel an already supported free reading.

Optional bread and tea trays cost two coins each, at most once per kind for this reading. Cash goes to the grocery or cafe till through an actual sale; existing personal bread tabs remain separate. The scene derives its refreshments from those exact payment receipts. This is one bounded spending purpose, not an unlimited job market or a complete sustainable economy. The public scene, actual player participation and money survive export, import and reload.

Kevin and Baba each contribute four existing business coins after payroll protection. Six are reserved for the three tasks; two remain for correction. Closing settles earned liabilities and returns all remaining unencumbered funds equally. The project never creates money, changes an old creditor or turns contribution credit into ownership.

## Player trust and access

Help, Journal, Inventory and destination navigation remain available after the opening conversation. Inventory supports eating directly and an optional shared meal with a present, explicitly accepted participant. A loaf is consumed once; its old debt remains payable. The meal records actual participants and nearby witnesses without an automatic affection reward.

Work now has a reserved full wage, employer-bound minutes and durable earned obligations. Suspension and midnight do not erase accrued progress or its hold. Imported ambiguous evidence stays labelled and needs explicit funded recognition; missing historical earnings are never invented. New work is a bounded unit with disclosed terms, rather than an unfunded recurring promise. Legacy valid balances and debts survive old normalization caps. There are no unexplained midnight top-ups or destructive till caps.

Origin checks and browser locks stop stale tabs overwriting a newer town. Explicit imports preserve a recovery copy before replacing bytes. Confirmation pauses movement and simulation during review, stops an old destination walk and supports keyboard cancellation. Replay cannot queue moves for the live town. Conflicts pause that copy and offer download/load actions. Movement saves periodically and when walking stops; unload saving remains best effort if the browser process is killed. Keep portable backups for important towns.

## Conversation and operation

Data and Amelia receive the current conversation partner's relationship. Authored local answers cover everyday concerns, roles, directions, work and known project outcomes. Recent dialogue, claims and recorded events retain their distinctions. Bounded archives keep complete stored records and pair attribution; important protected story/payment records remain. This is bounded recall, not a promise of permanent verbatim memory.

Background AI waits leave movement and town time responsive. Foreground replies allow walking, offer an immediate local reply and have a bounded wait. Leaving earshot invalidates the request and retains the draft. Town/request/context checks reject late or reordered output, including after import or a local-only switch. Local-only stops both direct and autonomous provider requests.

Both provider modes use the existing verified authentication boundary and shared budget reservations for deployed paid operation. The accounting includes conservative retry allowances, direct-reply capacity, per-user fairness and concurrency. Deployment needs the new shared-budget migration and explicit conservative tariffs; missing or failed accounting falls back locally. Development's finite process-local allowance is not a durable monetary guarantee. See [budget configuration](../../src/lib/mind-budget/README.md). No live database, provider keys, hosting or production configuration was changed for this implementation.

## Validation and remaining release gates

The [interruption and save-recovery receipt](../reviews/2026-10-16-interruptions.md) records the latest checks and reproduced fixes. The [preceding bug pass](../reviews/2026-10-16-bug-pass.md), [story refinement](../reviews/2026-10-15-first-reading.md), [earlier refinement](../reviews/2026-10-15-pre-release-polish.md) and [first implementation receipt](../reviews/2026-10-15-game-quality-delivery.md) preserve their dated commands, counts, screenshots and source revisions. Historical checks describe their earlier source and must not be treated as the candidate's release verdict.

Synthetic longevity checks reached 90, 180 and 365 game days at 56 exchanges per day. All checkpoints retained the 90-coin total and reimported their exports. The measured exports were approximately 1.10, 1.17 and 1.27 MB, with reimport taking 24, 21 and 25 milliseconds on this machine. Protected story/reflection history can still grow; these measurements do not prove an infinite town or physical-phone performance. Oversized exports fail visibly rather than silently deleting history.

The [five-person playtest](game-quality-playtest.md) remains a human evaluation gate. Physical-device keyboards, screen-reader usability, live provider quality/capacity, production tariff configuration and hosted publication require their own evidence. Automated sessions cannot substitute for those claims. The current architecture remains a browser-local town; account synchronization, background shared-world hosting, map expansion, banking and a general job market are outside this delivery.
