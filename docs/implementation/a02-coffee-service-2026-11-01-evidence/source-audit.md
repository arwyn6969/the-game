# Coffee serving and receiving: source audit

Date: 2026-11-01. Read-only audit of `codex/a02-loop` at `5c21468df22b83ecf95efa934913e05f5702da41`; production source remains the A02 base, `a26714e03192a17acaa963d7d2681e74a1ac425b`. This is implementation evidence, not a claim that coffee service exists. Only this report was added. Runtime, shared status, framework and A01 files were not edited; no runtime tests were run for this source review.

The existing town has useful custody, cash, movement and action patterns, but **no coffee order, prepared coffee item, recipient acceptance, handoff or consumption contract**. Coffee artwork and dialogue are not evidence of service. The smallest defensible addition is a bounded coffee-specific order/cup state with explicit collection; general gifting, a marketplace, token export and new resident spawning remain separate work.

## Space, movement and presentation

The café is built at `world.ts:130`, with its doorway at `(17,20)` and general anchor `(10,21)` at `world.ts:246`. The counters at `(5,19)`, `(5,20)` and `(6,19)` and tables at `(8,22)` and `(12,22)` are **blocked furniture** (`world.ts:190–194`). Existing art is not a reachable serving station. A future implementation must designate and test walkable staff and customer positions beside furniture. Do not use the café anchor alone as proof of preparing, serving or receiving a cup.

There is one normal resident movement pass (`sim.ts:224–232`). Library goals override physical-item goals, which override discussion goals. Manuscript travel also yields to active paid café attendance (`physical-items.ts:609–623`). Ordinary navigation has a three-step budget, real occupancy and no path through the player (`navigation.ts:9`, `32–38`, `101–149`). Coffee movement needs explicit placement in this priority system, an interruption rule, and actual-arrival checks. Running another movement loop for coffee would grant extra movement and invalidate the existing guarantees. Multiple staff cannot stand on one prep tile: capacity is bounded by actual reachable stations as well as money and accepted workers.

The visible cup on Kevin is currently selected from his public status text (`render.ts:223–230`), and cups/espresso machines are also scenery (`WorldArt.ts:338–345`). Neither is an inventory record. The model reflection prompt already says an offered cup does not establish that anybody drank it (`prompt.ts:519`). Keep that distinction in new receipts, memories and art: **offered, prepared, ready for collection, received and consumed are distinct recorded events**.

The existing player action UI is reusable. `ActionCards.tsx:15–31` renders native buttons with terms, visible disabled reasons and `aria-describedby`. `PlayerTools.tsx:403–445` supplies a live status region, but the inventory summary is presently hardcoded around loaves/manuscript. Its focus recovery at lines 79–85 should cover cup actions that disappear after consumption. `TownApp.tsx:722–733` rejects stale Town/panel callbacks, foreground activity and replay; successful actions persist immediately. A dedicated coffee panel or section can use these mechanisms and remain usable by keyboard/touch without dragging sprites or understanding a wallet. If coffee actions bypass `chooseAction`, mirror all those guards and failed-save feedback.

## Smallest useful new contract

This is a recommended boundary for the implementer, not an approved runtime schema or settled pricing policy:

1. A versioned coffee extension owns immutable order IDs, cup IDs and service action receipts. Each order names the payer, intended recipient, café, exact price, selected fulfilment method and current revision. Worker assignment is separate from the buyer/recipient. IDs are scoped to the persistent world; use existing actor IDs, never display names or wallet strings.
2. A successful funded order creates an obligation and its exact sale/reservation records, **not a completed cup**. Admit it only when the quoted queue/staff/station capacity and refund obligations can be supported. A worker then accepts exact funded preparation terms. No automatic staffing growth from a balance or text description alone.
3. Preparation records actual worker/location/work completion. It creates exactly one `cupId` for exactly one order, marked `ready` at a named service location and reserved to its recipient. Cup custody and buyer entitlement are separate: ready at the café does not mean held by the buyer.
4. Start with **collect at counter**. Show the intended recipient “Collect your coffee” when the named cup is ready and they are beside its valid service location. Acceptance atomically changes that exact cup from café custody to recipient custody and records receipt. The payer cannot accept for a different recipient; a resident recipient requires its own current deterministic consent or explicitly validated proposal. A “Buy X a coffee” offer may remain pending/declined without inventing acceptance.
5. Held coffee remains an untokenised item. “Drink coffee” consumes that exact owned/held cup once; subsequent use or collection fails or replays the original result without a new effect. Consumption neither charges again nor creates another wage. No automatic consumption simply because the cup was received. For the first slice, omit free ground drops, resale, cup transfer and NFT/stamp issuance.
6. Include cancellation/refund and uncollected-cup policy in the order terms. Close, reload, worker interruption or receiver absence must never silently destroy paid entitlement or earned wages. Keep “customer order fulfilled” separate from “preparation wage earned” and “cup consumed.” If ready-at-counter earns the wage, absent recipients do not reverse earned work. If hand delivery is promised, it requires a separate acknowledged handoff, reachable recipient and preserved entitlement on timeout.

Counter collection is smaller than waiter-to-customer handoff because it avoids chasing a moving recipient and avoids claiming another actor's acceptance. It still preserves the central analogy: paid demand funds accepted staff work, real staff prepare coffee, recipients can take and optionally drink it, and more supported demand can fund more work. Direct table service and gifting can extend the same IDs/receipts later after movement and consent policies are specified.

## Acceptance evidence to require

These are future acceptance cases; this audit did not execute or implement them.

- Exact-order/cup chain: payment alone creates no ready cup; preparation creates one; only the intended recipient in the valid area can collect; drink consumes once. Stale revision, another actor, changed recipient, mismatched cup/order and duplicate IDs leave state unchanged.
- Race and recovery: repeated command, double click, JSON reload, stale browser tab and interruption at each transition cannot duplicate payment, cup, wage, receipt or refund. Preserve the original invalid save if any domain record contradicts its ledger proof.
- Consent: a customer purchase, worker offer, spoken promise, visible cup animation and arrival at the café cannot individually fabricate worker acceptance, customer receipt or drinking. NPC acceptance/refusal is separately recorded.
- Geometry: blocked counter/table targets, occupied staff/customer positions, unreachable path, moving customer and existing commitments produce truthful waiting/interruption outcomes; nobody teleports and no extra movement pass runs. Add a second worker only with a funded accepted assignment and available station.
- Finance: protect legacy payroll, use available funds after holds, preserve accrued wages on customer absence, hold earned payable when a purse is full, and prove refund solvency independently of the preparation wage.
- Accessibility/offline: native labelled order/collect/drink buttons, disclosed terms, disabled reasons, live results, focus recovery after one-use actions, replay inhibition and useful offline behavior; no wallet required.
- Compatibility: ledgerless v1 import, pre-coffee v2 import, current manuscript custody, bread debt/consumption and all eight resident identities survive. Optional missing coffee state means no coffee orders, not fabricated old stock.
