# First day, on the screen

The opening line tells you the clock speed, how to walk, and which way Hobbs is. It does not say what anyone wants.

Talk is a panel with the close control fixed at the top and the line you are typing fixed at the bottom. On a phone the panel is a sheet, not the whole screen, so those controls stay in reach. What you see is what you heard and what is still owed. Private wants, plans, and retrieval scores stay behind the developer inspector, which is not written into the town.

A small amber mark points toward Hobbs on the first day, until you are close. It is a direction, not a secret.
