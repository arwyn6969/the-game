# Library meeting and opt-in listening

The first meeting reuses the reading event's library venue, actual travel/arrival and voluntary participation patterns. It has its own optional versioned record; existing authored readings and their receipts retain their original meaning.

Proximity makes listening possible. A resident must deliberately attend to this meeting to receive its words. Invitation delivery, acceptance, arrival, listening and speaking are separate. NPC choices and speech use the existing optional, background-accounted action capability, with no authored substitute if it is unavailable. Tests use explicit synthetic model transport, never production credentials or provider calls.

The pilot supports Northern, Arwyn and Sub with optional player participation, one topic, one current speaker and a finite action/turn allowance. Northern remains the default proposed facilitator; a local operator trial may instead propose Arwyn through the optional engine parameter. A proposed facilitator must still accept and deliberately attend. An agent can decline, listen silently, pass or leave. One shared transcript records actual recipients at each utterance commitment. Late arrivals learn future turns; a later recap is a new statement. Watching a transcript as a spectator does not grant character knowledge or attendance.

The maximum initial earshot is six horizontal-plus-vertical tiles, subject to acoustic boundaries. Walls separate interiors; furniture does not. This limit does not select listeners. A listening target is a meeting/conversation ID, not blanket permission to hear everything a resident says later.

Speech is a statement, not proof that an action happened or an agreement was accepted. Payment, work and project changes remain behind their existing explicit validated actions. Meeting decisions cannot control other actors or expose their private memories.

Local verification must cover opt-out nearby residents, declined/missing invitations, actual arrival, late join/leave during a pending reply, duplicate/retried results, save/reload/import/replay, unchanged old readings and economy, and usable keyboard/touch controls at 1280×800 and 390×844. Real-provider qualification and hosted background allocation are separate from the local implementation. Production currently disables background AI; this feature must not borrow the player-only budget to bypass that policy.

The task deliberately excludes a new hosting platform, production quotas/secrets/migrations, automatic away-time simulation, voting or spending, and a separate asynchronous forum product.
